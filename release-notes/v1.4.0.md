# Storage Manager 1.4.0

The Database tab is easier to find your way around, every figure leads to its list, and the panel works with the keyboard, in light and dark themes and on phones.

- **A jump bar and folding sections.** The Database tab starts with its health and the recommendations, then the entity list. Dead entities, statistics nobody needs and events fold away with their count in the title. The bar under the header leads to each part.
- **Figures open their list.** The database size opens all entities by size, the global retention opens the settings, and the recommendations in the overview open the matching part of the Database tab.
- **Clear where numbers come from.** The detail sheet says which figures come from the last analysis and which were read just now.
- **Keyboard, contrast and touch.** The treemap and the time range work with the keyboard (the range can be typed), texts and buttons have enough contrast in both default themes, and buttons are big enough for a finger on phones.

Also: the nightly limit for long-term statistics checks the database again before it deletes when the last check could not run. See the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md) for details.
