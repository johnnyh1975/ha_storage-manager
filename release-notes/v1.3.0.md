# Storage Manager 1.3.0

What you see in the preview is now exactly what gets deleted, and Storage Manager no longer touches your `configuration.yaml`.

- **Your `configuration.yaml` stays yours.** To stop recording entities, Storage Manager needs packages. The repair issue used to add the line for you; now it shows the two lines to add yourself, checks that they are there when you confirm, and then writes only its own package file.
- **Preview and cleanup are bound.** A cleanup deletes only what its preview showed: the same entities, statistics, events, local backups and file categories. What appeared in the meantime is left alone, also new events of a type that is still recorded. A preview older than 15 minutes deletes nothing and asks you to open it again.
- **One list for everything.** The estimate, the targeted backup for undo and the deletion use the same selection, so the backup always covers what is deleted.
- **Undo lasts longer.** It stays available even after the history is shortened.
- **Lowering the global retention asks first** and says how much history Home Assistant's next nightly purge will delete, without undo.

Also: a cleanup that fails halfway stays in the history with its Undo; retention runs no longer overlap with a cleanup; the analysis is saved less often and survives a reload; a repack waits for an integrity check that ran; unreadable energy settings no longer make meters look unused; dialogs keep the keyboard focus inside; events that are still recorded are marked; crash dumps are recognised by their content; integration names show everywhere; the singular is used for one item. Inside, the cleanup and the undo have their own modules and the panel is tested in a browser in CI.
