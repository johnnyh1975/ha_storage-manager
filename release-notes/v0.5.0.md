# Storage Manager 0.5.0

## Retention savings in the overview
"Biggest savings" now lists the open recommendations for shorter retention with the space they free, for example "Shorter retention for 34 entities · 1.2 GB". This replaces the old hint "10 entities = 45 % of the database", which named a size, not a saving. The header shows both figures: what can be freed safely right away, and what shorter retention adds.

The space from shorter retention is freed by the nightly purge. The database file only gets smaller after a repack.

## Rules by kind of sensor
A new card under *Database → Rules by kind of sensor* lists every kind Storage Manager recognises: diagnostic values, time and date, power, energy meters, GPS trackers, presence and distance, attribute-only updates, large attributes and very frequent values. For each kind you see how many entities match, how much history they hold and what the rule saves. You can choose another tier or switch the recommendation off.

- The rules only change the recommendations. Nothing changes in the recorder until you accept and apply it.
- Long-term statistics stay: power and energy meters cannot be set to "don't record". For other kinds, entities with statistics keep 1 day instead.
- Use in dashboards, helpers and the energy dashboard is still checked for every entity.

See [docs/sensor-rules.md](https://github.com/johnnyh1975/ha_storage-manager/blob/main/docs/sensor-rules.md) for the rules and their sources.
