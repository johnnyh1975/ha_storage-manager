# Storage Manager 0.8.0

Two gaps that people have asked about for years in the community thread on keeping the recorder database small, plus better protection.

## Statistics nobody needs
Home Assistant never deletes long-term statistics. A Wi-Fi signal or uptime sensor with a `state_class` adds 24 rows a day, forever. The new card in the database view lists:
- **signal, uptime, IP, firmware and time sensors** that no energy dashboard or statistics card shows. *Switch off and delete* removes their `state_class` through `homeassistant: customize:` in Storage Manager's package and deletes the statistics. After the next restart no new statistics are compiled; whatever was compiled until then is cleared on start and every night. The state history stays.
- **disabled entities**: they record nothing new, but their statistics stay forever.
- **imported statistics** without a new row for 90 days, for example from a removed energy provider.

Battery, temperature, humidity, voltage, power, energy and current keep their statistics even on diagnostic entities. Switched-off statistics can be switched back on (after a restart; deleted statistics do not come back).

## Old events by type
Excluding an event type only stops new rows; the old ones stay until the purge after the retention period, which can be months or years. The events card now deletes the recorded events of a type right away, in chunks on the recorder thread, after the safety chain. `state_changed` is never offered, and types the logbook shows are marked.

If `system_log` has `fire_event: true`, every log entry becomes a `system_log_event` row. That, and many old rows of that type, now shows up as a recommendation.

## Better protection
- **YAML helpers**: `statistics`, `history_stats` and `filter` sensors from YAML or packages protect the history of their source, just like UI helpers.
- **YAML files**: mentions of an entity in the YAML files of the config folder (packages, templates, scenes) are listed with file and line. Templates that loop over a whole domain (`states.sensor`) are pointed out.
- **Statistics cards** on dashboards protect an entity's long-term statistics.

## Fixed
Purges are now waited for until the rows are really gone. `recorder.purge_entities` returns as soon as the purge is queued, and large purges queue themselves again in batches, so the freed space and the analysis could run ahead of the purge.
