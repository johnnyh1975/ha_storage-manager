## Rules by kind of sensor
Storage Manager now recognises common kinds of sensors and recommends what suits them. Long-term statistics are always kept, so the energy dashboard and statistics cards are not affected.

| Kind | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Power (W, more often than every 15 min) | 3 days |
| Energy meters (kWh) | 7 days |
| GPS trackers | 7 days |
| Time and date sensors | don't record (1 day with statistics) |
| Presence and distance sensors (mmWave) | 1 day |

The existing rules (diagnostic values, attribute-only updates, large attributes, very frequent values) stay.

## Many small sensors count together
Until now each entity had to be worth it on its own. Hundreds of small sensors of one kind – Powercalc is the typical case – never got a recommendation, even when together they take a gigabyte. Rules are now judged per integration. Four or more recommendations of the same kind from one integration appear as one line in the inbox; open it to see the entities. Usage protection still applies to every single entity.

## Throttle at the source
Sensors that report more than once a minute get a hint how to send fewer values in the first place – ESPHome filters, Powercalc update intervals, Shelly reporting intervals. That reduces writes, not only stored history.
